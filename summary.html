Arrhythmia atlas (lead II)

Sinus rhythms

Normal sinus rhythm

P before every QRS, PR 0.12–0.20 s, QRS ≤ 0.10 s, regular, 60–100 bpm.

L10 p15

Sinus bradycardia

Normal ECG, rate below 60. Normal in athletes; also carotid sinus syndrome.

L11 p9

Sinus tachycardia

Normal ECG, rate above 100. Fever (about +10 bpm per °F), sympathetic drive, toxins.

L11 p9

Blocks

SA block

A whole P-QRS-T missing; the rhythm restarts from the SA node.

L11 p11

First-degree AV block

PR fixed and > 0.20 s; every P conducts.

L11 p13

Mobitz I (Wenckebach)

PR lengthens until a QRS drops, then resets. AV node; usually benign.

L11 p13

Mobitz II

PR constant; some P waves not conducted. His bundle or bundle branches.

L11 p13

Third-degree (complete) block

P and QRS unrelated; slow escape rhythm (wide if ventricular). Stokes-Adams attacks.

L11 p14

Early beats

Premature atrial contraction

Early beat with its own P wave and a normal QRS; PR may be short.

L12 p4

Premature ventricular contraction

Early wide QRS, no P, T opposite the QRS, compensatory pause.

L12 p5

Ventricular bigeminy

A PVC after every normal beat (trigeminy: every third beat).

L12 p5

Fast and lethal rhythms

Atrial fibrillation

No P waves, fibrillating baseline, irregularly irregular, narrow QRS. Reentry; foci in the pulmonary veins; stroke risk.

L12 p6–7

Atrial flutter

Regular sawtooth F waves at 200–350/min; usually a fixed AV ratio (2:1 to 4:1), though conduction can vary; narrow QRS. One circus wave in the right atrium.

L12 p8

Ventricular tachycardia

Run of fast, wide, uniform QRS complexes. P waves are usually not seen, but they can march through independently (AV dissociation).

L12 p9

Torsades de pointes

Polymorphic VT twisting around the baseline; long QT; low K+, Mg2+ or Ca2+.

L12 p9–10

Ventricular fibrillation

No P, QRS or T waves. No cardiac output: defibrillate within minutes.

L12 p11–12

Asystole

Flat line: no electrical activity.

L12 p13

WPW, QT and ischemia

Wolff-Parkinson-White

Short PR (< 0.12 s), delta wave, wide QRS (> 0.12 s). Bundle of Kent bypasses the AV node.

L12 p14

Long QT

QT long by both the 10–11 small-box range and the rate-corrected QTc; risk of torsades.

L12 p10

ST elevation

Transmural infarct (STEMI pattern).

L15 p19

ST depression / T inversion

Subendocardial ischemia (NSTEMI pattern).

L15 p19

Pathologic Q waves

Q deeper than 1/3 of the QRS: a usually lasting change after MI. Full criteria also consider Q width and which leads show it.

L15 p21

U waves

Small wave after T; prominent in bradycardia and hypokalemia.

Jugular venous pulse patterns

Normal

a (atrial contraction), c (tricuspid closes), x′ (ring pulled down in systole), v (atrial filling), y (tricuspid opens).

L13 p16

Large a wave

RV hypertrophy from pulmonary hypertension, pulmonary stenosis or tricuspid stenosis.

L13 p17

No a wave

Atrial fibrillation: no atrial contraction.

L13 p17

No y descent

Pericardial tamponade: the atrium cannot empty into the compressed ventricle.
